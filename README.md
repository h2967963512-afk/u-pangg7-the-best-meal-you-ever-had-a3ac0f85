## The best meal I ever had

> At 2 a.m. in a Taipei alley, I ate beef noodle soup standing up, broth burning my tongue, and the old man behind the counter refilled my bowl without asking, like he'd been waiting for me.

## Why it stays with me

It was not a fancy restaurant or a planned occasion — just a tiny stall, late at night, steam in the cold air, and a bowl of soup that tasted like someone had been perfecting it for decades. What made it unforgettable was the refill: no words, no bill padded with ceremony, just an old man who saw a hungry stranger standing in his alley and decided he had not eaten enough yet. That quiet generosity turned a snack into a memory I still think about years later.
